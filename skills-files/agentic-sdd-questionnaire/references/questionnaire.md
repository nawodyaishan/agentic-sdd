# Discovery questionnaire structure

Use only for a recipient artifact. Reuse the agreed audience and purpose rather
than requiring the user to fill a universal intake form.

```text
# <Topic> discovery questionnaire

Purpose: the decision this knowledge informs.
From / To: actual known roles or names; unknown where not supplied.
How answers will be used: owning requirements/design source and confirmation owner.

## Context
Enough relevant background to answer without the original conversation.

## How to answer
Known deadline/effort, if any. Partial answers and “I don't know” are useful.
Please identify assumptions, uncertainty and the source of observed facts.

## <Consequential theme>
### <One clear question>
Why it matters: include only if needed to make the question useful.
Answer / uncertainty / source:

## Anything else?
What material gap, conflict or risk have we missed?
```

Before delivery, compare each requested knowledge gap with its question. Do not
invent an answer or silently treat a blank as confirmation. Do not include
credentials, private account records or unnecessary personal/project information.
Sending/contacting the recipient is a separate user decision.
