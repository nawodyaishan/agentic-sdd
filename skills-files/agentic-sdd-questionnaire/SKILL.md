# Agentic SDD Questionnaire

Read the [shared policy](../agentic-sdd-router/references/workflow-policy.md) once
per unchanged context. Use the [human-input contract](../agentic-sdd-router/references/human-input.md)
for decision meaning and [questionnaire structure](references/questionnaire.md)
when drafting a recipient artifact.

## Prepare the questions

1. Identify the blocked decision and knowledge gap. Investigate permitted
   discoverable facts first; use this procedure when a distinct recipient holds
   needed knowledge, not to shift routine research to the user.
2. Reuse known recipient role/expertise, relationship, purpose and intended use.
   Ask only missing send-context questions; do not interrogate the user about
   facts they have already said only the recipient knows. Do not invent names,
   deadlines or an established requirement owner.
3. Cover every material requested gap with one clear question, context as needed
   and an answer/uncertainty stub. Prioritize consequential questions and group
   by theme. Check coverage against the original gap list before finishing.
4. Create the artifact only when requested or justified by its distinct consumer
   and current write authority, using the repository's established document
   location and a new non-colliding name. Otherwise return the draft in chat.

Drafting is not permission to send, contact a person, share project information
or start implementation. The user decides delivery and disclosure.

## Reconcile answers

Attribute recipient/source/date and stated confidence; mark missing dates or
uncertain facts unknown. Separate observed facts, preferences and suggestions.
Preserve conflicting answers and name who must resolve each consequential one.
Propose traceable requirement changes in the relevant spec and design inputs in
the plan; the user or established requirement owner confirms their adoption.
An answer alone cannot approve a feature, waive a finding or authorize a batch.
In review-only mode report proposals without editing canonical state.

Finish with coverage, unresolved inputs and the next permitted action. Keep the
questionnaire only when it still serves its recipient; do not introduce a second
approval ledger or a mandatory questionnaire for ordinary work.
